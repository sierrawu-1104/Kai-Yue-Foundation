Donation requests are received in written physical OR PDF format and are by invitation only.

Eligibility

  • Application for donation is by invitation only. KAI YUE FOUNDATION does not accept non-solicited applications.
  • Typical donation amounts are from $500 to $20,000.
  • Applicant must have a current and active registered charitable number (Canada) or 501(c)3 status (US).
  • Funds must be utilized in the states where organizations provide services.
  • Organizations must be applying for programs and services that fall in line with one of the foundation's areas of focus.
  • Organizations must have strong fiscal management based on information gathered from the most recent copy of their financial statements.
  • Organizations must have an operating Board of Directors and be able to illustrate the strength of their governance practices.
  • Organizations must provide an annual progress report and feedback on how funds are used and the effectiveness of the program.
  • Presentations and site visits may be required.
